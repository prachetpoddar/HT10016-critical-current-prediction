`analysis/a10_on_repaired_cohort.py`, deposits `audit/a10_baseline_trace.csv`

## Stage 1, which every ratio divides by

**Refuted, mine.** I was going to write that the descriptor carries no signal
and Stage 1 is not a baseline. The first half is wrong and refutable from the
deposit in one line.

Stage 1's declared purpose in that file is rank order, and on rank it works:

| held out | actual rank | predicted | error | magnitude residual |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| iron_pnictide_122 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1.32 |
| conventional_AlB2 | 2 | 3 | 1 | 1.98 |
| iron_chalcogenide_11 | 3 | 4 | 1 | 6.23 |
| cuprate_LSCO | 4 | 4 | 0 | 10.81 |
| cuprate_RBCO | 5 | 5 | 0 | 20.00 |
| cuprate_BSCCO | 6 | 5 | 1 | 20.00 |
| iron_pnictide_1111 | 1 | 4 | 3 | 25.71 |

Rank mean absolute error 1.00, six of seven within one position.

What was never validated is the magnitude, and the magnitude is the
denominator. The mean is 12.29. Two of the seven targets are exactly 30.000,
the value the field fitter is bounded at rather than a measurement, and their
residuals of 20.002 each are 46 percent of it. Three families, all cuprates,
carry the identical descriptor value 3.44 with targets of 30.00, 5.50 and
30.00, so no fit can separate them. For `iron_pnictide_1111` the fold predicts
27.2 against an actual 1.50.

**The clean statement needs no correlation test.** Stage 1 loses to a constant.
Against the out-of-sample median of every training fit, which uses no
descriptor, no family and no sample form, the Stage 1 regression is worse by
1.37, 2.36 and 2.34 times on the three cohorts. A denominator beaten by a
constant cannot measure what conditioning is worth.

## Conditioning against a constant

| cohort | arm | pooled_forms | pooled_flat | nearest_family |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| all seven families | deposited | 0.59 | 0.61 | 0.78 |
| physicality ok | deposited | 0.82 | 0.92 | 0.63 |

| physicality ok | repaired | 1.49 | 0.98 | 0.68 |
| any | admitted | 1.77 | 1.47 | 0.77 |

Above 1 means conditioning beats the constant.

**Refuted, mine.** I was going to write that this is 12 of 12 and that
conditioning does not reduce the error at all. Four corrections.

There are not twelve comparisons. `nearest_family` and `nearest_no_form` are
numerically identical on every fold, and no cuprate fit passes physicality, so
the physicality-passing cohort and the cuprates-removed cohort score the same
four families. Three readings and two family sets: six.

It is not stable. Removing one family moves the deposited ratios between 0.09
and 1.87, and most combinations cross 1.

Scored per held-out fit rather than per family median, which is what a reader
would assume, the ratio is 1.00 with a 95 percent interval of [0.91, 1.11] on
the physicality-passing cohort and 0.98 with [0.97, 0.99] with the cuprates
removed. Indistinguishable from a constant, not below it.

And the admitted cohort wins, up to 1.77 on the 52 fits the stated protocol
admits, which is the cleanest data in the deposit and the arm I was about to
omit.

## Why none of it can settle the question

Sample form is nearly nested inside substructure in this corpus:

| | bulk | polycrystal | single crystal | thin film | wire |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| conventional_AlB2 | 10 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 10 |
| cuprate_BSCCO | 14 | 0 | 0 | 6 | 0 |
| cuprate_LSCO | 5 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| cuprate_RBCO | 3 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| iron_chalcogenide_11 | 0 | 0 | 37 | 14 | 0 |
| iron_pnictide_1111 | 0 | 15 | 3 | 4 | 0 |
| iron_pnictide_122 | 0 | 8 | 27 | 3 | 0 |

Wire occurs in one family. Bulk is 22 of 32 cuprate, and 28 of 154 fits sit at
the exponent bound of 30. Holding a substructure out therefore removes most of
its forms from training, so the form-restricted pool for `conventional_AlB2` is
22 cuprate bulk fits with a median at the ceiling, and that single fold supplies
about a quarter of the error the reading is charged with. Leave-one-substructure-out
cannot separate a form effect from a family effect on this corpus, which is the
same confounding Sec. III.A concedes for the temperature axis, appearing here in
the validation design rather than in the diagnostic.

## The defensible position

Neither scope is distinguishable from a constant on this corpus, and the design
cannot separate sample form from substructure. That is what the letter should
say, and it is close to what `final9` already says: report the two errors and
the family count, and no quotient.

## One thing to resolve before submission

Sec. III.A of the manuscript states "the descriptor carries genuine but weak
rank information, Spearman rho = 0.635 with a 95 percent confidence interval of
[0.061, 0.948]". That does not reproduce from
`data/phase_3_p18_substructure_descriptor_means.csv` under any framing filter:
the seven-family value is 0.408 with p = 0.364 and a bootstrap interval of
[-0.647, 1.000]; the three-family `Hc2` framing gives 0.500; all ten rows give
0.431. A Fisher interval around 0.635 matches [0.061, 0.948] at about eleven
observations, and there is no eleven-family cohort. The per-fold training
correlations in `phase_3_p39_stage1_loso_rank_order.csv` are 0.030, 0.334,
0.406 and 0.941, none of them 0.635.

The 12.30 in the same paragraph reproduces exactly. The 0.635 does not, and the
two sit three sentences apart.

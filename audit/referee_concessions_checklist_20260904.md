recomputed, and four are broken because the evidence offered for them is itself
defective. The broken four are not the peripheral ones: A1 is the deposit the
referee was invited to check, A3 is Referee A's central physics objection, A9 is
the dataset-size ladder, and A10 is the largest retraction the letter makes.

Nothing here reopens a concession in the authors' favour. Every one of the four
was conceded correctly and is now, if anything, conceded on evidence weaker than
the letter presents.

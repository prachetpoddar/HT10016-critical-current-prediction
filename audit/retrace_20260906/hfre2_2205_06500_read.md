# 2205.06500v1 read against its printed figure

Recorded 2026-09-06, closing the last open verification item. The full
repository was searched first: this source appears in exactly two places, the
retrieval corpus `data/caption_sweep.csv`, where it scores zero on every family
flag, and the Table II input. It is in no fit cohort, no provenance table and no
audit disposition, so it was never screened and nothing had been read.

## What the deposit holds

`HfRe2`, 128 rows, all from page 25 of `2205.06500v1.pdf`. Four temperatures,
2 to 5 K. Two pressures, 0 and 1.10 GPa, carried in `compound_raw` as "2 K,
0 GPa" and so on. Sixteen field values per temperature and pressure, running
1e-6 to 1e-5 T, and the same sixteen values at every temperature and pressure.
The literature critical field attached to the compound is 10 T, so the largest
reduced field in the whole record is 1e-6. The published Table II fit gives it
a Form 3 field exponent of 2.87e6.

## What the paper says

The source is real and the extraction did read it: the paper's Figure 8 plots
critical current density at P = 0 GPa in panel (a) and P = 1.10 GPa in panel
(b), which are the extraction's two pressures exactly.

Two things do not match.

**The compound is wrong.** The paper studies **Re6Hf**, a non-centrosymmetric
superconductor. The deposit records HfRe2, a different stoichiometry and a
different structure. The family assignment and the critical-field lookup both
follow from the label. By coincidence the number survives: the paper reports an
orbital critical field of 9.78 T at ambient pressure, so the 10 T in the
deposit is about right for Re6Hf.

**The field axis is wrong by about six orders of magnitude.** A critical-current
figure for a superconductor with a critical field near 9.78 T plots fields of
order a tenth of a tesla to a few tesla. The deposit records 1e-6 to 1e-5 T.
The sixteen field values are also identical across every temperature and
pressure, which is a generated grid rather than a reading of sixteen plotted
markers.

## Disposition

This is the same defect class the screen confirmed in every one of the eighteen
sources it read, and closest to `1002.0208v2`, whose "field column spans 1e-6 to
1.6e-3 T" against a printed axis of 0 to 16 T.

It touches Table II and nothing else. HfRe2 appears in no fit cohort, so no
published exponent, count or validation figure moves. Table II's cohort is the
pre-screen dataset and already contains all eighteen screen-failed sources;
this is a nineteenth that was never examined. The functional-form conclusions
were rerun with the eighteen removed and held, at probabilities between 0.001
and 0.004 for Form 3 over Form 1 and no separation from Form 2, so removing a
nineteenth compound would not move them either.

No manuscript number changes. What changes is that the last unexamined source
in the Table II cohort has now been examined, and it failed.

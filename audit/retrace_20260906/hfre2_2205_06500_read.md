## The extraction is a reading of the right paper

The abstract describes a polycrystalline non-centrosymmetric superconductor
studied under pressure, with magnetization at about 1.10 GPa and an analysis of
the critical current density "using the collective pinning theory" that shows a
transformation from delta-Tc to delta-l pinning as pressure rises. The deposit's
128 rows carry `compound_raw` labels of the form "2 K, 0 GPa" and
"2 K, 1.10 GPa" over isotherms at 2, 3, 4 and 5 K. The pressure series and the
pressure value match the paper. Whatever is wrong here, the extraction was
looking at this paper, which is not the case for the six field-axis fits
withdrawn from `s41467-025-55880-4`.

## The compound label is wrong

The paper studies **Re6Hf**. The deposit records the compound as **HfRe2**.
These are different materials: Re6Hf is the alpha-Mn-structure
non-centrosymmetric phase the paper is about, and HfRe2 is a Laves phase. The
recorded transition temperature of 5.6 K is consistent with the Re6Hf
literature, so the label is wrong while the sample is right, which is the same
class of defect as the La2FeAsO for LaFeAsO substitution Referee A found, one
level worse because it changes the stoichiometry rather than the notation.

## The field column cannot be a reading of any field axis

The 128 rows span 1e-6 to 1e-5 T over 16 distinct values. That is **0.01 to 0.1
oersted**. No critical-current measurement sweeps a field axis over a tenth of
an oersted; the Earth's field is about 0.5 oersted. Against the recorded
critical field of 10 T the largest reduced field in the compound is 1e-6, which
is why the Form 3 fit for this compound returns a field exponent of 2.87e6 with
its standard error, and why the conditioning diagnostic flagged it.

The signature is the one the withdrawal ledger names for `1612.02839v1`: "over
fields of 1e-6 to 1e-4 T, which are the log-axis minor ticks with the decade
shifted". A shift of 1e-4 from a plausible 0.01 to 0.1 T axis reproduces this
column exactly. That is the likely mechanism and it is not established here,
because establishing it needs the figure.

## What this costs

provenance row and no audit disposition. It reaches the paper only through the
Table II input, which is the pre-screen dataset, and Table II's conclusions are
unchanged with the eighteen screen-failed sources removed. The record should be
corrected or withdrawn before the deposit is archived, and the compound label
is wrong whatever happens to the field column.

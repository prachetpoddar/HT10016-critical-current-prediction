# The SmFeAsO field axis is kilo-oersted, confirmed at source

Recorded 2026-09-04, from the full text supplied this session. Nothing in the
data changed yet.

Tamegai, Nakajima, Tsuchiya, Iyo, Miyazawa, Shirage, Kito and Eisaki,
*Magneto-optical imaging of iron-oxypnictide SmFeAsO(1-x)Fx and SmFeAsO(1-y)*,
Physica C 469 (2009) 915-920. The corpus held only page one. Pages two to six
were supplied.

**Fig. 2 plots Jc in A/cm2 against H in kilo-oersted, 0 to about 50 kOe**, with
nine series at 2, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35 and 40 K. The inference recorded
earlier today from the repeated 46.055 ceiling was right.

Every series terminates in the data where its curve terminates on the page, and
the paper's own sentence, "the value of Jc at low fields is about 2 x 10^5
A/cm2 at T = 5 K", matches the extraction's 2.28e5. The only defect is the unit:
kilo-oersted numbers were written into a `field_T` column.

## What the correction does

Dividing the fields by ten takes the measured span from 0.53 to 0.053 of the
86 T Tier 3 default. The applicability criterion of Eq. (1) of the main text
requires the span to exceed 0.3 of the upper critical field, so:

| | before | after |
|---|---:|---:|
| passing field-axis fits from this paper | 8 | **0** |

Separately, the paper states **Tc = 43 K** while the deposit records
`Tc_K_anchor` of 55.0, which is the literature value for the family rather than
this sample.
